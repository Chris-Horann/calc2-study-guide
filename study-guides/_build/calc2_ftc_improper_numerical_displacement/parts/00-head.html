Not covered here

Only sections that appear in your notes are included. §1.2 (trig integrals and trig substitution), §2.1–§2.3, §3.2 (area in the plane) and §3.4 (polar coordinates) aren't in your notes, so they're left out. From §3.3, arc length for $x=f(y)$, arc-length parameterization and the polar exercises aren't in your notes either. From §3.5, the cylindrical shell method isn't in your notes yet: they stop at disks and washers.

Also left out: textbook-only material your notes don't use, such as initial-value problems, cyclic integration by parts ($e^x\sin x$), comparison tests, error-bound formulas, average velocity, and surface area (§3.6).

How to use it

  • Attempt before you reveal. Hints unlock one at a time after an “I'm stuck” check-in. The full solution waits until you've tried (or you ask twice).
  • Answers accept exact forms (23/3, 2sqrt(3)-2, pi/4, 3cbrt(2)) or decimals. For improper integrals you can type diverges. Antiderivatives are typed as expressions (x*e^x - e^x + C, ln(abs(x)), arctan(x)) and are checked up to the constant, so any correct form is accepted. You're told when you forget $+C$ or an absolute value.
  • Ask your tutor boxes build a prompt you can paste into Claude. They follow the “I'm stuck” rules in your CLAUDE.md, so you get a nudge instead of the answer.
  • Your progress and notes are saved in this browser only. Every answer in this guide was checked three ways: by hand, with Python (SymPy/mpmath), and with Wolfram Language.
