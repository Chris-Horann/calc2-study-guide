⚠ Discrepancies found and how they were handled
  • Notes p. 10 (integration by parts): the last line of $\int2t^2e^{-t}\,dt$ reads $-4t^{-t}$, but it should be $-4e^{-t}$, as the page's own side work shows. The guide uses the corrected answer $-2(t^2+2t+2)e^{-t}+C$ and flags the slip where the example appears (§1.1 parts: Compare and Practice 3).
  • Notes p. 18 (partial fractions): cosmetic slips that don't affect the answer. The numerators are labeled $Ax+B$, $Cx+D$ although the variable is $r$, and “④ − ②” should read “④ − ③”. Noted in §1.3 Practice 4.
  • Notes p. 37: the Trapezoid Rule with $n=4$ for $\int_{-1}^1\sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx$ is written as $\tfrac12+\tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4}\approx1.468$. The correct value is $\tfrac12+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\approx1.366$. The guide uses the verified value and flags it where it appears (§2.6, guided example).
  • Textbook §2.6 Additional Ex. 9 asks for Simpson's Rule on data at $t=0,2,\dots,14$. That is 7 subintervals (odd), so Simpson's Rule can't be applied as stated, and the exercise isn't used.
  • New notes p. 7 (a reading note, not an error): in $\vec v(t)=(3t^2,t,0)$ the $t$'s crossbar runs through the 3, so it looks overwritten. It matches the other 3s on that page, so the guide reads it as 3.
  • A tool disagreement, resolved: SymPy returned a wrong value for $\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin4t|\,dt$ (4 instead of 16). The guide's figure for the helix tryout, $32+6\pi\approx50.85$, was confirmed hump by hump, with mpmath, and with Wolfram.
  • All six worked answers on new notes pp. 1–3 are correct.
  • 3.3–3.5 notes (rest3.3-3.5.pdf): all four worked answers are correct: 28 m (p. 1), $\frac{74}{3}$ and $\pi$ (p. 3), and $\pi(\sqrt2+1)$ (p. 4). Two reading notes, neither an error. First, p. 1 writes $\sqrt{(t+4)^2}=t+4$ without the absolute value, which is valid because $t+4>0$ on $[1,5]$. Second, p. 5 has a red handwritten mark over the printed label $\Delta x_i$ in a pasted textbook figure; its intent is uncertain, and it doesn't affect any formula. The two volume examples on pp. 6–7 are left blank in the notes; their answers here ($\frac{32\pi}{3}$ and $8\pi$) were checked three ways, and against the cone formula.
